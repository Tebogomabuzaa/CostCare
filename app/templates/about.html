{{ icon("users") }} About CostCare

Healthcare costs, made predictable.

Travellers often struggle to find accurate, current healthcare prices — and end up with surprise bills. CostCare puts clear price estimates in front of you before you visit a provider.

Our mission

Give every traveller and resident a reliable estimate of healthcare costs so they can budget with confidence and get care without financial surprises.

Our vision

A world where healthcare pricing is as transparent as booking a flight — starting in South Africa and expanding across the continent.
